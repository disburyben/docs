---
title: "Onboarding your Hermes agent"
description: "Pick an inference provider and model, connect Telegram, and authorize your account"
---

Once Hermes is deployed, click **Open in Hostinger** to access the chat. Sign in with the admin
username and password you saved during [VPS setup](/hermes/vps-setup).

## Step 1: Choose an inference provider

Many providers are available, but the cheapest non-open-source option is **OpenAI Codex**. This
lets you use your existing ChatGPT subscription ($20, $100, or $200/month) instead of paying per
API call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-in URL">
    Use the URL Hermes provides.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize access">
    Approve the connection in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the 9-digit code">
    Paste it back into the terminal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Choose a model

<Card title="Pick GPT-5.5" icon="microchip">
  It offers the best balance of capability and cost for most workflows.
</Card>

## Step 3: Set up Telegram

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Telegram">
    Hit **space** to select Telegram, then **enter** to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a bot with BotFather">
    In Telegram, open **BotFather** and send `/newbot`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your bot">
    Give the bot a name and a unique username.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the bot token">
    Copy the bot token and paste it into the VPS terminal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 4: Authorize your Telegram account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your user ID">
    Search for the **USERINFO** bot in Telegram and message it to get your user ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the home channel user">
    Paste your user ID into the terminal as the home channel user.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the home channel">
    Confirm **yes** when asked if this is the home channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 5: Save your setup info

Hermes shows you your tool availability, settings paths, API keys location, and configuration
paths.

<Warning>
  Copy **all** of this and paste it into your [Claude Code tracking project](/hermes/managing-agents).
  You'll need it later when troubleshooting.
</Warning>
